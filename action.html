Move the needle

Turn what you learned into action.

Reducing stigma doesn't require having every answer. Being willing to listen, using respectful language, and helping people find support are actions we can practice.

01 / Your next step

Three actions you can try.

Choose one realistic way to help make supportive conversations more common.

01 / LISTEN

Give someone space

If a friend opens up, listen without judgment. You don't have to immediately offer solutions.

02 / SPEAK

Challenge hurtful labels

Don't repeat jokes or comments that make asking for help seem embarrassing or weak.

03 / CONNECT

Encourage support

If someone needs more help, encourage reaching out to a counselor, trusted adult, or health professional.

02 / Why this project exists

Awareness matters when we share it.

The goal is to reach people beyond one classroom and encourage conversations that are kinder and less judgmental.
